Inspired by the TED talk of percussionist, Evelyn Glennie, the piece emphasizes on the raw sounds of a conventional instruments, that is only audible to musicians. This performance included the breathing and valve sounds coming out of trumpet. Quadrophonic, for trumpet and electronics.
