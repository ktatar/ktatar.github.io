A performance with the Intonarumori (Luigi Russolo’s noise machines) at the Vancouver Art Gallery on April 12th. 
The <i>intonarumori</i> are a group of experimental musical instruments created by the Italian futurist Luigi Russolo in the early 1900s. Local musicians bring these instruments to life with casual, improvisational performances.<br><br>

**Performers:**  Kıvanç Tatar,&nbsp;<a href="https://matthewariaratnam.wordpress.com/">Matthew Ariaratnam</a>, <a href="https://afivepence.wordpress.com/" target="_blank">Alex Spence</a>, <a href="http://cargocollective.com/alexmah" target="_blank">Alex Mah</a>, <a href="http://www.benwyliemusic.com/" target="_blank">Ben Wylie</a>, and <a href="https://rebeccabruton.wordpress.com/" target="_blank">Rebecca Bruton</a>
